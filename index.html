01
Day by Day Sample Itinerary
1

Arrival Day

Settle in · First sunset · Lake vibes

Afternoon

Check in to your hotel

Stay inside Old City — near Lal Ghat, Gangaur Ghat, or Ambrai Ghat. Everything is walkable from here.

Evening

🚡 Karni Mata Ropeway — Sunset

The best sunset spot in Udaipur. Go just before sunset but stay after dark — that's when the colors really show. Full city view from the top. It's quiet. Unreal.

Night

🚤 Pichola Lake Boat Ride

Take two rides — one at golden hour, one after dark to see city lights from the lake. ₹185/ride.

2

City & Lake Day

Palaces · Hills · Boat · Sunset

5:00 AM

🌄 Bahubali Hills — Sunrise

30 min from city by scooty. Easy 10-min trek to the top. Wide, calm, unreal views. Leave by 5 AM to catch 6 AM sunrise.

Morning

🏰 Sajjangarh Fort (Monsoon Palace)

Breathtaking Aravalli views. Entry ₹110. Best shots from the terrace.

Afternoon

🏛️ City Palace + Ambrai Ghat

Ask for "Garden Ticket" at City Palace — ₹50 only (skip the ₹400 ticket). At Ambrai Ghat, sit facing the palace, put your feet in the water. Closes 8 PM.

Evening

🏯 Jag Mandir Boat Ride

After 3 PM: ₹700 (best for sunset view from middle of lake). Last boat at 5:15 PM from City Palace. Don't eat there — food is overpriced. Just enjoy the walk.

3

Ranakpur & Kumbhalgarh

Day trip · Temples · Fort walls

Early AM

Start early — it's a full day

Ranakpur is ~95 km from Udaipur, then another 50 km to Kumbhalgarh. Best done by scooty or rental car.

Afternoon

🕌 Ranakpur Jain Temple

1,444 carved marble pillars — opens for tourists in the afternoon. Free entry for Indians. Light filtering through carvings is stunning.

Evening

🏰 Kumbhalgarh Fort — The Great Wall of India

Entry ₹40. Panoramic views and a majestic feel. Stay to see the fort light up at dusk.

4

Old City & Farewell

Walk · Temple · Brunch · Goodbye

Morning

🛕 Jagdish Temple

Intricately carved temple near City Palace. A beautiful architectural marvel — free to visit.

Late AM

🏯 City Palace Museum

Takes 2–3 hours. A proper look into Mewar royal history. Don't rush it.

Noon

🍽️ Brunch with a lake view

Find a café overlooking Lake Pichola. Jheel's Ginger Cafe for Nutella pancakes or veg exotic pizza.

Best Spots Sunsets & Sunrises
🌅 Karni Mata Ropeway Best of all
  • Go just before sunset — stay after dark, that's when colors really pop
  • On top of a hill — full 360° city view
  • For sunrise: walk up via temple steps (not ropeway), follow track to city wall
  • Left side: Lake Pichola · Right side: Sunrise over Aravallis
🌊 Ambrai Ghat
  • Peaceful. Sit facing the City Palace
  • Put your feet in the water
  • Stay till dark — the City Palace lights up beautifully
  • Closes by 8 PM
🏯 City Palace Garden
  • Ask for "Garden Ticket" — ₹50 only (not ₹400)
  • After sunset, walls glow gold and sky turns deep blue
  • Perfect golden-hour photo spot
⛰️ Bahubali Hills — Sunrise
  • Leave city by 5:00 AM for 6:00 AM sunrise
  • 30 min from city by scooty
  • Easy 10-min trek to top
  • Wide, calm, unreal views
  • Scooty rental: ₹350–₹370

Romantic Spots Lakeside Restaurants
💡 For all three restaurants below: book in advance — weekends fill up fast. Weekdays are much easier to get a table.
🕯️ Ambrai Restaurant Must Pre-book
  • No online bookings — call directly
  • Side lake seats: ₹2,000/person cover
  • Middle seats are free and almost the same view — smart choice
📸 Jagat Niwas Palace Restaurant
  • Most Instagrammable spot — entire walls are white
  • Wear dark colors — looks best in photos
  • Sunset is the best time for pics
  • Skip food — just order Coke or coffee and chill
🌊 Upre Restaurant
  • Right beside Ambrai, same stunning lake view
  • Almost always booked — pre-book it

Practical Tips The Basics
✈️

Airport → City

No Ubers at airport. Book a rental car. Cost: ₹350–₹600. Cars can't enter old city lanes.

🛵

Getting Around

Scooty rental is the move. ₹350–₹450/day. Rental shops everywhere in Old City.

🏨

Where to Stay

Old City only. Near City Palace. Lal Ghat, Gangaur Ghat, Ambrai Ghat area.

💧

Essentials

Carry water. Take breaks every 1–2 hrs if riding. Keep all docs handy.
